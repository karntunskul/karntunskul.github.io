About

I am on the job market for 2026–2027.

Hello, I am Karn. I am a PhD candidate in the Department of Agricultural, Environmental, and Development Economics (AEDE) at The Ohio State University.

I am an economist and an environmentalist (not always in that order). My research focuses on environmental and resource economics, with the current focus on land conservation, forest and agricultural land use, and land-based environmental policies. My work experience in the Thai government — particularly at the Department of Climate Change and Environment and the Cooperative Promotion Department — has shaped my interests and ambitions for policy-relevant research.

Outside of research, I enjoy playing games (video and tabletop), assembling puzzles, going for walks in the park when it’s warm, and trying new food and restaurants.
